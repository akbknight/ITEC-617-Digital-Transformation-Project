# ITEC-617 Digital Transformation Project Coach

## Project Overview

This is an LLM-powered interactive simulation for MBA students in AU Kogod ITEC-617 (Information and Technology). It helps students prepare their Digital Transformation team project presentations by challenging them from multiple executive perspectives.

## How to Use

- **Project Setup**: Use `/setup-project` to interactively fill in your project brief through guided Q&A
- **Persona Skills**: Use `/cio`, `/cfo`, `/ciso`, `/coo`, `/chro`, `/cto`, `/cdo`, `/legal`, `/procurement` to consult executive personas
- **Evaluation**: Use `/evaluate` to score your proposal across 9 rubric dimensions (45 criteria)
- **Presentation Prep**: Use `/presentation-prep` for mock Q&A with a simulated judge panel

### Signals>Noise Custom Commands

These three commands are configured specifically for the Signals>Noise team (IT Expert, Telecom Specialist, International Business Developer, Financial Analyst) working on a Hospitality/Hotel Industry DT proposal.

| Command | Purpose | When to Use |
|---------|---------|------------|
| `/business-summary` | Translate any technical output (Python/R code, financial model, research) into a plain-English Business Value Summary focused on ROI and strategic value. Saves the file to `documentation/business-summaries/` for OneDrive sharing with non-technical teammates. | After generating any technical content, or when you need to brief a non-technical teammate |
| `/hospitality-research` | Run targeted research on your configured Target Company and Emerging Technology through three lenses: FAAAM competitive threats, Gartner Hype Cycle positioning, and NASA Technology Readiness Levels. Reads configuration from `research/active-research.md`. | Before finalizing your technology choice, or to build the competitive context section of your proposal |
| `/tech-validate` | Run a four-part technology validation — maturity, business fit, organizational readiness, and risk profile — scored against the rubric criteria judges will use. Produces a decision recommendation and predicts likely judge challenges. | Before committing to your technology choice, or to stress-test your proposal before `/evaluate` |

**Note**: `/business-summary` also runs automatically whenever technical code or complex research is generated. The auto-generated summary appears at the end of the AI response — you can then save it to `documentation/business-summaries/` for your team.

## Content Directories

- `primer/` - 16 reference sections: 15 Enterprise IT Primer + industry judge advice (markdown, for LLM context and student reference)
- `rubrics/` - 9 evaluation dimensions, 45 criteria (markdown, for scoring and feedback)
- `student-workspace/` - Project brief, presentation notes, ROI summary, OCM assessment templates
  - `student-workspace/project-proposal-part1.md` - Part 1 structured proposal placeholder (company, problem, technology, strategic alignment)
  - `student-workspace/project-proposal-part2.md` - Part 2 deep-dive placeholder (financials, OCM, architecture, vendor, security, roadmap)
- `student-workspace/source-docs/` - Students drop prior assignment deliverables here for AI context
- `research/` - Industry Research Hub for Hospitality/Hotel Industry
  - `research/active-research.md` - Configurable research file: set target company and technology to get tailored FAAAM, Gartner, and NASA TRL analysis
- `documentation/business-summaries/` - Plain-English Business Value Summaries generated by `/business-summary`; copy files here to team OneDrive for non-technical teammates

## Student Workflow

1. Drop any prior assignment deliverables (PDFs, DOCXs) into `student-workspace/source-docs/`, then run `/setup-project` to interactively fill in your project brief (`student-workspace/project-brief.md`). The setup flow detects your current assignment milestone and adapts its questions accordingly. For ROI work, use `student-workspace/roi-summary.md`; for OCM, use `student-workspace/ocm-assessment.md`.
   - **Signals>Noise**: The proposal-part files (`project-proposal-part1.md` and `project-proposal-part2.md`) are separate, more detailed structured templates — fill them in manually after completing `/setup-project`, or use them in place of the standard project-brief.md. They contain hospitality-specific sections not in the default brief.
2. Set your target company and technology in `research/active-research.md`, then run `/hospitality-research` to generate tailored FAAAM, Gartner, and NASA TRL research.
3. Run `/tech-validate` to stress-test your technology choice before committing to it in your proposal.
4. Consult executive personas using slash commands (`/cio`, `/cfo`, etc.). The CFO also reads `roi-summary.md` and the CHRO also reads `ocm-assessment.md` when those files have content.
5. Run `/evaluate` after 3-4 persona consultations to see your scores (also considers ROI and OCM companion files)
6. Address gaps by consulting personas who cover your lowest-scoring dimensions
7. Once you have draft slides, place a **copy** of your `.pptx` in `student-workspace/slides/` and run:
   ```
   uv run extract_presentation.py
   ```
   Always keep your original PowerPoint in a separate folder. Use `git commit` to snapshot your work before each AI feedback session.
8. Run `/presentation-prep` for mock Q&A practice
9. Use `/business-summary` at any time to generate a plain-English summary of technical work for your non-technical teammates. Files are saved to `documentation/business-summaries/` — copy them to your team OneDrive.
