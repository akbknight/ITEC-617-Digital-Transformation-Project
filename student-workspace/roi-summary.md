## Benefit Categories

### Revenue Impact

- New advisory-led engagement revenue from pre-procurement workshops and paid diagnostics.
- Higher attach of AT&T solution bids due to improved requirement alignment.
- Quantified impact: $950,000 in Year 1; $1,450,000 in Year 2; $1,900,000 in Year 3.

### Cost Savings

- Lower pursuit cost on low-fit opportunities through earlier disqualification.
- Reduced manual pre-sales and bid strategy effort for poorly scoped opportunities.
- Quantified impact: $200,000 in Year 1; $350,000 in Year 2; $500,000 in Year 3.

### Efficiency Gains

- Faster qualification and decision cycle for bid/no-bid decisions.
- Reduced rework in solution scoping due to clearer requirement framing.
- Quantified impact: $150,000 in Year 1; $280,000 in Year 2; $420,000 in Year 3.

### Risk Reduction

- Lower legal and procurement challenge exposure via cleaner requirement documentation and governance controls.
- Reduced opportunity cost from misaligned deal pursuits.
- Quantified impact: $80,000 in Year 1; $180,000 in Year 2; $340,000 in Year 3.

## Key Metrics

- **ROI**: (($6,800,000 - $4,770,000) / $4,770,000) x 100 = **42.6%** (3-year base case)
- **Payback Period**: **Approximately 24 months** (cumulative benefits exceed cumulative costs during Year 2)
- **NPV** (if applicable): **Approximately $1.46M** at a 10% discount rate (base case)
- **Funding Type**: **Both**. Pilot-year spend is primarily OpEx (software and services), with selective CapEx treatment for reusable internal assets.

## Key Assumptions

*List every major assumption behind your numbers. The CFO will scrutinize these.*

1. Pilot adoption reaches 70% active use within 6 months and 85% by end of Year 2.
2. Advisory-to-core attach uplift contributes a 2.5%-4.0% win probability increase on in-scope deals.
3. Program launches on schedule (4-week setup plus 12-week pilot) with no critical delay above 6 weeks.
4. Governance controls (advisor-bidder separation, legal review gates) remain intact and do not materially slow conversion.
5. Cloud and model usage costs increase no more than 10%-12% annually under planned utilization.
