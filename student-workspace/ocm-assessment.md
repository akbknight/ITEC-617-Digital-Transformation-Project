## Rogers' Five Factors of Innovation Diffusion

*For each factor, rate whether it favors adoption (Yes), works against adoption (No), or is mixed (Mixed). Be honest — acknowledging challenges shows maturity and helps you plan mitigations.*

### 1. Relative Advantage

**Rating**: [x] Yes  [ ] No  [ ] Mixed

*Is the proposed technology clearly better than what it replaces? Do stakeholders perceive the advantage?*

**Assessment**:

The solution has a clear relative advantage for both leadership and field teams. Leadership gains earlier influence in enterprise opportunities, while pre-sales teams gain better qualification clarity and reduce low-fit pursuit effort. Compared to the current reactive model, the advisory model improves win strategy quality and reduces wasted pursuit cycles.

**Evidence**:

- Proposal baseline shows substantial effort spent on poorly scoped or low-probability RFP pursuits.
- Financial model projects 42.6% base-case ROI over three years, with measurable quality and conversion improvements.
- Industry trend supports shift toward advisory-led enterprise selling in complex infrastructure decisions.

**If Mixed or No — Mitigation**:

Not applicable; however, pilot will still run a control group to validate advantage with hard evidence.

### 2. Trialability

**Rating**: [x] Yes  [ ] No  [ ] Mixed

*Can people try it before fully committing? Is the pilot structured to let stakeholders experiment safely?*

**Assessment**:

The pilot is designed as a contained trial in one enterprise segment with 25-30 opportunities and a matched control group. Users can test workflows with real opportunities while retaining fallback to existing bid processes.

**Pilot Design**:

- Scope: one enterprise segment, pre-sales and solution engineering teams
- Duration: 12 weeks live pilot plus 4-week setup
- Processes: early diagnostics, scenario review, requirement shaping support
- Success criteria: uplift in opportunity quality and win indicators versus control group, with compliance and neutrality guardrails maintained

**If Mixed or No — Mitigation**:

Not applicable; trial design is explicit and reversible.

### 3. Compatibility

**Rating**: [ ] Yes  [ ] No  [x] Mixed

*Is the technology compatible with existing systems, workflows, values, and culture?*

**Assessment**:

Technical compatibility is moderate to good due to API-based integration with CRM and workflow systems. Process and cultural compatibility are mixed because teams must shift from reactive bid execution to consultative, upstream engagement. This is a significant behavior and incentive change.

**Integration Points**:

- CRM opportunity workflows
- Pre-sales qualification and solution design processes
- Legal/procurement review gates
- Sales leadership cadence and performance reviews
- Existing quota and compensation norms

**If Mixed or No — Mitigation**:

1. Introduce role-specific process redesign with clear RACI.
2. Align incentives to include advisory-quality behaviors.
3. Run manager coaching playbooks and weekly reinforcement.

### 4. Observability

**Rating**: [x] Yes  [ ] No  [ ] Mixed

*Can people see the results? Are the benefits visible to stakeholders beyond the pilot team?*

**Assessment**:

Pilot outcomes are highly observable through dashboards and operating reviews. Teams can see cycle-time changes, qualification improvements, and rework reduction in near real time. Leaders can monitor both performance and guardrail metrics.

**Communication Plan**:

Results will be shared through weekly KPI snapshots, monthly leadership readouts, and short success stories from pilot users. Both quantitative and qualitative evidence will be communicated.

**If Mixed or No — Mitigation**:

Not applicable; observability is built into pilot reporting.

### 5. Complexity

**Rating**: [ ] Yes (Low Complexity)  [ ] No (High Complexity)  [x] Mixed

*Is the technology easy to understand and use? How steep is the learning curve?*

**Assessment**:

User interface complexity can be managed, but workflow complexity is moderate because teams must interpret diagnostics and communicate advisory recommendations to clients. Learning curve is meaningful for sales and pre-sales staff used to traditional RFP response motions.

**Training Plan**:

- Audience: account executives, solution engineers, pre-sales architects, sales managers
- Format: blended learning (self-paced modules, instructor sessions, live simulation labs)
- Duration: 8-12 hours initial training plus weekly office hours during pilot
- Support: change champions and manager coaching sessions

**If Mixed or No — Mitigation**:

1. Use phased feature rollout (diagnostics first, then scenario design, then output templates).
2. Maintain mandatory human approval and simple recommendation templates early.
3. Deploy super-user network for rapid support.

## Stakeholder Impact Assessment
